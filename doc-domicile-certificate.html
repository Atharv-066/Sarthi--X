Domicile Certificate

Proof of residence in Maharashtra, required for state-quota college admissions and several scholarships.

⏱️
15–21 days
Processing time
💵
₹33 (self-attested + stamp)
Govt. fee
🏢
2 visits
Office visits

Step-by-step guide (Amravati)

1

Collect and fill the application form

Get Form 16 from the Tehsil office or download it, and fill in your residential history.

🏢 Office
Tehsil Office, Amravati
👤 Meet
Clerk, Citizen Facilitation Counter
📝 Form
Domicile Certificate Application (Form 16)
⏱️ Time
20 min
2

Submit with supporting documents

Attach residence proof and the notarised self-declaration affidavit at the submission counter.

🏢 Office
Tehsil Office, Amravati
👤 Meet
Naib Tehsildar
📝 Form
Form 16 + Affidavit
⏱️ Time
15 min
3

Field verification (if required)

A revenue official may visit your residence to verify the address on record.

🏢 Office
Your residence, Amravati
👤 Meet
Talathi (Village-level officer)
📝 Form
—
⏱️ Time
3–5 days
4

Approval and collection

Once verified, the Tehsildar signs off and the certificate is issued at the counter or via Aaple Sarkar portal.

🏢 Office
Tehsil Office, Amravati
👤 Meet
Tehsildar
📝 Form
—

1–2 days
